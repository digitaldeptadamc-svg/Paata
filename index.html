Department of Dravyaguna
Laghu Paata
Botanical Name :

  • Cissampelos pareira L

    • Family Name :

  • Menispermaceae

    • Local Name :

  • Padavaliballi

    • Vernacular Name :

  • Hindi – Patha

  • English – Velvetleaf

    • Synonyms :

  • Ambashtha

  • Pathani


    • Habitat :

  • Found in tropical and subtropical regions of India. It commonly grows in forests, hedges, wastelands, and along roadsides as a climbing or trailing plant.


    • External Morphology :

  • Stem: Slender, soft, hairy, twining or trailing climber.

  • Leaves: Simple, alternate, broadly ovate to heart-shaped, soft and hairy.

  • Flowers: Small, greenish-yellow, unisexual and arranged in clusters.


    • Useful Parts :

  • Root


    • RasadiPanchaka :

  • Rasa – Tikta, Kashaya

  • Guna – Laghu

  • Vipaka - Katu

  • Virya - Ushna


    • Pharmalogical Action :

  • Krimighna

  • Jwaraghna

  • Pachana


    • Therapeutic Administration:

  • Jwara (Fever): Root decoction is traditionally used.

  • Krimi (Worm Infestation): Root powder is traditionally administered.


    • Dose

  • Churna: 2–3 g

  • Svarasa: 10–20 ml

  • Kwatha: 50–100 ml


    • Formulation:

  • Pathadi Churna

  • Pathadi Kwatha
